SNapp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: SNapp is a Windows app that saves lecture slides from your screen. Your slides are stored on your own computer. If you choose to connect Google Drive, SNapp uploads your slides to a SNapp folder in your Drive. SNapp has no servers of its own, collects no analytics and sends nothing to its developer.

1. What SNapp stores on your computer

  • Captured slides as PNG images in Documents\LectureSnap\<date>\.
  • Settings (schedules, startup choice) in your Windows user registry.
  • If Google Drive is connected: a sign-in token encrypted with Windows (DPAPI), so only your Windows account can use it, and a small upload queue. These are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LectureSnap\.

Screen images are only taken while capture is running (started by you or by a schedule you set) or when you press Capture Now.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you click Connect Google Drive, you sign in on Google's own page. SNapp never sees your password. SNapp requests only these permissions:

  • drive.file: to create the SNapp folder and upload your slides into it. With this permission SNapp can only access files and folders it created itself. It cannot see, change or delete anything else in your Drive.
  • openid and email: to show which Google account is connected (for example, “Connected as you@gmail.com”). This is shown only inside the app on your computer.

Data from Google is used only to provide this backup to you. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is not transferred to anyone else, including the developer.

SNapp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Phone remote

The phone remote is a small web page served by SNapp on your own computer, reachable only on your local network and only with the pairing code shown in the app. It shows capture counts and small previews of your latest slides. Nothing goes through the internet.

4. What SNapp does not do

  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any server other than Google (only if you connect Drive).

5. Removing your data

  • Disconnect Google: SNapp Settings → Disconnect. This revokes SNapp's access and deletes the token. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Slides in Drive stay in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps. Your saved slides in Documents are kept, and you can delete them yourself.

6. This website

The SNapp download page uses Google Analytics to count visits, downloads and video plays (for example, which country visitors come from and which button was clicked). This is about the website only — the SNapp app itself sends no analytics. You can block it with any ad/tracker blocker, or opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

7. Children

SNapp is intended for students and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/moniruzzamanmoonna/SNapp/issues.
